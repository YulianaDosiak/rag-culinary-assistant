DINNER in 30 minutes

One-pan Mexican Quinoa
1 TB olive oil, 2 cloves garlic, 1 jalapeño, 1 C quinoa, 1 C vegetable broth, 1 can black beans, 1 can diced tomatoes, 1 C frozen corn, 1 tsp. chili powder, ½ tsp. cumin. Heat oil, cook garlic/jalapeno. Stir in rest, bring to boil, cover and simmer 20 mins.

Swift Stir-Fry
1-2 chicken breasts strips, 1/4 C + 2 TB Mr. Yoshida's Sauce, 16 oz. frozen stir-fry vegetable mix, 1/2 C water, 2 tbsp. cornstarch. Marinate meat, cook vegetables 3-4 mins. Sautee chicken 5 mins. Whisk 1/4 cup sauce, water, cornstarch, add to chicken to thicken. Stir in cashews, add vegetables.

Broccoli Chickpea Stir-Fry
2 tbsp. olive oil, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 1½ heads broccoli chopped, 1 can chickpeas, 1/3 C broth, 1/4 tsp. crushed red pepper, 1/3 C parmesan cheese. Sautee onion/garlic 4-5 mins. Add broccoli for 3 mins. Add chickpeas, broth, red pepper. Cover and cook 3-9 mins. Sprinkle with parmesan.

Baked Potatoes
1 potato per person. Poke holes, microwave in glass dish or wrap in foil and bake at 400° for 1 hour. Add toppings.

Ham and Cheese Oven Sandwiches
1 Rhodes "Bake n Serve" loaf, Deli ham, Mozzarella cheese. Let dough rise all day. Roll out, put ham/cheese in one layer. Bake at 350° for 20-25 mins.

Tuna Casserole
Noodles cooked al dente, 1 can cream of chicken/mushroom, 1 can tuna, Milk (start with ½ of soup can). Drain noodles, return to pot. Stir in soup, tuna, milk. Heat and serve.

Pasta
Skinny Alfredo Sauce: 12 oz fettuccine, 1 Tbsp olive oil/butter, 4 cloves garlic, 3 Tbsp flour, 1 cup chicken stock, 1 cup low-fat milk, 3/4 cup Parmesan, salt/pepper. Sauté garlic in oil, whisk in flour, broth, milk. Simmer to thicken, stir in Parmesan, combine with pasta.
Pesto Tortellini: 6-20 oz tortellini, bottle of pesto, grilled chicken. Cook tortellini, mix with chicken and pesto, heat through.

Fish
How to Cook Most Types of Fish: Bake at 350°F (thawed) or 400°F (frozen) for 15-20 mins. Pan Fry over medium heat for 5-7 mins each side.

Tacos or Burritos
How to Cook Taco Meat: Bake chicken at 375°F for 35-40 mins, or pan fry ground beef. Season with taco seasoning (1/3 cup minced onion, 2 tsp salt, 2 tbsp chili powder, 2 tbsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp oregano, 2 tbsp flour).

Lentil Tacos
½ tbsp. olive oil, 1 cup chopped onion, 1 clove garlic, 1 cup dried brown lentils, 1 package taco seasoning, 2½ cups water. Heat oil, cook onion/garlic. Add lentils/seasoning, cook 1 min. Add water, bring to boil, cover and simmer 25-30 mins until liquid is gone.
